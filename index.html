    How this comparison works

    DiffLens compares complete lines. An edited line appears as a removal followed by an addition. It does not execute your code.

    Windows and Unix line endings are treated equally. A final newline is significant and may appear as a blank-line change. Character-level highlighting and file uploads are not included.
